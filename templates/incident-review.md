# Incident: <short title>

## Impact and evidence

Affected versions, platform, user-visible failure, and reproducible evidence. Distinguish confirmed impact from suspected impact.

## Cause and missed check

Technical cause, the decision or process that allowed it, and why existing checks did not catch it. Do not use “human error” as the complete explanation.

## Correction and validation

Fix commit, regression test, tested artifact hash, actual environment, and verification results. Record the failing case before the fix and passing behavior afterward.

## Prevention beyond this incident

Identify related failure classes and the gate or procedure covering each. Assign an owner, completion criterion, and follow-up date to remaining work. Add a negative test proving each gate rejects bad input.

## Communication

Affected release, corrected release, upgrade guidance, and known limitations. Keep completed internal incident records local and remove secrets from any public summary.
